# Ticket release rules

- Early Bird price: NGN 4,000 per individual admission.
- Early Bird allocation: 30 individual admissions in total.
- Regular: NGN 8,000 for one admission.
- Friends of 5: NGN 36,000 for five individual admissions.
- The later categories are unlocked only after all 30 Early Bird admissions have paid and been verified.
- Temporary unsubmitted reservation: 20 minutes.
- Submitted payment review hold: 24 hours.
- The database counts admissions, not order rows. Friends of 5 produces five independently verifiable tickets.

These are the current assumptions encoded in the project. Confirm them with the organiser before opening sales.
